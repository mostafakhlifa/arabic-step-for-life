Newcomer Support

Welcome home. We help immigrants and newcomers settle in, find their footing, and feel part of the community from day one.

A Warm Welcome

Starting over in a new country takes courage. You don't have to do it alone.

Arriving somewhere new means learning new systems, a new language, and a new way of everyday life — often while carrying the hopes of your whole family. That is an enormous act of courage, and it deserves a warm welcome.

Our newcomer programs offer orientation, practical guidance, language support, and friendship — so new neighbors can settle in with confidence and start building the life they came here for.

How We Help

Settling in, step by step

Orientation & Guidance

Friendly guidance on everyday life in the United States — schools, healthcare, transportation, housing, and local services.

Language Support

Help building English confidence for daily life, alongside Arabic learning that keeps families connected to their heritage.

Work & Daily Life

Support navigating job searches, resumes, and workplace culture — and practical help with the paperwork of a new life.

Family & School Support

Guidance for parents navigating the school system, so children can thrive in their new classrooms and communities.

Community Connection

Introductions to neighbors, community events, and cultural gatherings — because belonging starts with knowing someone.

A Listening Ear

Moving to a new country can feel lonely. Our volunteers offer friendship, encouragement, and a community that cares.
