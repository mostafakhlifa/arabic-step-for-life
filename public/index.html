Who We Are

A community that shows up — with open arms and open hearts.

Arabic Step for Life is a United States nonprofit organization built on a simple belief: no one should face healing or a new beginning alone. Whether someone is recovering from a burn injury, arriving in a new country, or hoping their children grow up connected to their language and roots, we are here to walk beside them.

Our volunteers, partners, and supporters form a caring community that offers practical help, emotional support, and genuine belonging.

Our Mission & Vision

Why we exist

Our Mission

To support burn survivors in their physical and emotional recovery, to welcome and empower immigrants and newcomers as they build new lives, and to teach the Arabic language as a bridge between generations and cultures — with dignity, compassion, and respect for every person we serve.

Our Vision

A community where every burn survivor feels seen and supported, every newcomer feels at home, and every family can pass on its language and heritage with pride — a place where healing, belonging, and hope grow together.

Volunteer With Us

Your time can change someone's life.

Volunteers are the heart of Arabic Step for Life. Whether you can mentor a newcomer family, help organize a community event, tutor an Arabic student, or simply lend a listening ear to someone recovering — there is a place for you here.

No special experience is needed to start. We provide orientation and ongoing support, and every hour you give strengthens our community.
