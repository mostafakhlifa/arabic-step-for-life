Ways to volunteer

Newcomer Mentors

Welcome a newcomer family, help them navigate daily life, and be a friendly face in a new city.

Arabic Tutors & Teachers

Teach or tutor Arabic classes for children and adults, or help in the classroom.

Burn Support Companions

Offer companionship and encouragement to survivors and families during recovery.

Event Helpers

Help organize and run community gatherings, fundraisers, and awareness events.

Interpreters & Translators

Bridge language gaps for families navigating appointments, schools, and services.

Skilled Volunteers

Share professional skills — design, photography, writing, administration, or technology — to strengthen our work.
