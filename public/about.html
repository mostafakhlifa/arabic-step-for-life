About Arabic Step for Life

Healing, Belonging & Hope — the three words that guide everything we do.

Our Story

Born from compassion, grown by community.

Arabic Step for Life Organization, Inc. was founded by people who saw neighbors facing extraordinary challenges — a long recovery after a burn injury, the disorientation of arriving in a new country, the quiet worry of parents watching their children drift from their language — and decided that nobody should face those moments alone.

What began as small acts of help between neighbors has grown into an organization serving burn survivors, immigrants and newcomers, and families who want to keep Arabic alive — all across the United States. Our name reflects our promise: we take each step of life's journey together, in Arabic and in English, in hardship and in hope.

Our Mission

To support, empower, and create opportunities for individuals and families in need.

Our Vision

A more inclusive community where everyone can heal, belong, and thrive.

Our Values

Compassion, Inclusion, Respect, and Opportunity.

What We Stand For

Our values

Compassion First

Every person who comes to us is met with warmth, patience, and respect — never judgment. We listen before we act.

Dignity in Support

Help should lift people up, not hold them down. We work with the people we serve, honoring their choices and strengths.

Belonging for All

Our doors are open to everyone, regardless of background, faith, or circumstance. Diversity is our strength.

Heritage & Learning

Language carries memory, identity, and love. Teaching Arabic is how we help families stay rooted while they grow.

Community Power

Lasting change happens when neighbors help neighbors. Volunteers, partners, and donors make every program possible.

Hope in Action

We believe recovery is possible, new beginnings are possible, and every small step forward deserves to be celebrated.
