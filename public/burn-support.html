Burn Survivor Support

Recovery is a long road — and no one should walk it alone. We stand beside burn survivors and their families with practical help, emotional support, and community.

You Are Not Alone

Healing takes time, courage — and people who care.

A burn injury changes life in an instant. Beyond medical treatment come months of healing, adjustments at home and work, and the emotional weight of it all — for survivors and for the families who love them.

Our burn support programs offer a steady hand through that journey: someone to talk to who understands, help navigating daily challenges, and a community that sees the person — not the injury.

How We Help

Support for every step of recovery

Emotional Support

A compassionate ear and peer connection for survivors and families coping with trauma, change, and the long road of healing.

Practical Assistance

Help with everyday needs during recovery — from navigating appointments and paperwork to connecting with local resources.

Family Support

Families carry so much during recovery. We support caregivers and loved ones too, so the whole family can heal together.

Community Connection

Meeting others who have walked a similar path can be profoundly healing. We help survivors find community and belonging.

Awareness & Advocacy

We raise awareness about burn prevention and the needs of survivors, so communities become safer and more welcoming.

Rebuilding Confidence

Recovery includes rediscovering confidence and joy. We celebrate every milestone, big and small, along the way.

Looking for support?

If you or a loved one is a burn survivor and could use support, we invite you to explore our burn support programs and apply online. Create a free account, tell us about your situation, and our team will review your application and reach out.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please call emergency services (911 in the United States) or go to your nearest emergency department. Arabic Step for Life provides community support, not medical treatment.
