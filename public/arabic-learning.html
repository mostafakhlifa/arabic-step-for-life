Arabic Learning

Language is where heritage lives. Our Arabic classes help children and adults read, write, speak, and stay connected to their roots.

Language Is Belonging

When children speak their grandparents' language, a whole world stays open.

For many families, Arabic is the language of home — of grandparents' stories, holiday gatherings, and prayers. But in a new country, it can quietly slip away from the next generation.

Our Arabic learning programs make the language come alive: patient teachers, joyful classes, and a community of learners who encourage each other. Whether you are starting from the alphabet or polishing your reading, there is a place for you here.

What We Offer

Learning for every age and level

Children's Classes

Fun, engaging Arabic lessons for children — letters, sounds, words, and stories — designed to build confidence and a love of the language.

Adult Learners

Classes for adults at every level — from complete beginners reconnecting with their heritage to advanced learners refining their skills.

Reading & Writing

Step-by-step progress in reading and writing Arabic script, with plenty of practice and patient guidance along the way.

Conversation Practice

Real-life speaking practice in a friendly setting — because a language truly comes alive when you use it with others.

Culture & Heritage

Stories, songs, and traditions woven into learning, so students grow in language and in pride for their heritage.

Family Learning

When families learn together, the language lives at home. We encourage parents and children to practice side by side.
